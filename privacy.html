Privacy Policy

Full Moon Flow is designed from the ground up to protect your privacy.

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

Your data never leaves your device. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we couldn't share it even if someone asked.

What data the app stores

Full Moon Flow stores the dates and flow intensity (spotting, light, medium, or heavy) that you manually enter. This data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. No other personal information is collected — no name, no email, no device identifiers, no location.

What data is transmitted

None. Full Moon Flow makes zero network requests. There are no servers, no APIs, no analytics services, no crash reporting tools, and no advertising SDKs. The app functions entirely offline.

Data export

You can export your logged data as a CSV file using the in-app export feature. This uses your device's native share sheet — the app does not control where you send the file. The exported CSV contains only dates and flow intensity values.

No accounts

Full Moon Flow does not have user accounts, login screens, or any form of authentication. There is nothing to sign up for and no way to identify you.

Data deletion

Your data exists only on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data. There are no backups, no cloud copies, and no way to recover the data after deletion.

Third-party services

Full Moon Flow uses no third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that process user data. The app includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

Full Moon Flow does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app collects no data from anyone — all information remains on the user's device.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Since the app collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact: bliplogapp@gmail.com
